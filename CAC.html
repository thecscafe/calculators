Calculate your CAC

Add your total acquisition spend for a given period and the number of new customers you won in that same period. The result shows your average Customer Acquisition Cost per new customer.

Tip: Use one full quarter of data so the number is stable enough for planning.

After you get your CAC, compare it with your LTV and payback period. If CAC is high relative to lifetime value, your growth may look good on paper but crush margin and CS investments.
