Calculator library

Pick a calculator, plug in your numbers, then save the output for your next planning doc or leadership review.

  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Calculator

    See what you really spend to win each new customer.

    Add your sales and marketing spend, then compare CAC against payback period and LTV. Useful for CS leaders who partner with Sales and Marketing on efficient growth and want to argue for better post-sale investment.

    Calculate CAC

  • Lifetime Value (LTV) Calculator

    Measure long term revenue per account.

    Estimate LTV using average revenue and retention patterns. Use it to show why keeping and growing customers through CS often beats net new logo spend and to connect your work directly to revenue quality.

    Calculate LTV

  • Net Revenue Retention (NRR) Calculator

    Track how well you keep and grow existing revenue.

    Input starting ARR, expansion, downsell and churn to see NRR in a few clicks. Great for CS teams that want to separate logo churn from revenue churn and explain what is really happening in the base.

    Calculate NRR

  • Revenue Physics Calculator

    See which forces are pushing your ARR forward or slowing it down.

    Model how new business, expansion, contraction and churn work together. Use this when you need to explain to leadership why NRR is stalling even though new sales look strong.

    Calculate Revenue Physics

  • Tech-Touch ROI Calculator

    Check if automation is creating real revenue, not just busy work.

    Compare the cost of tech-touch programs with the expansion, retention and ticket reduction they drive. Helpful when you argue for CS tooling, in-app guidance or AI programs that scale outcomes without bloating headcount.

    Calculate Tech-Touch ROI

  • 5-Point Revenue Impact Scale

    Score where each initiative really sits on the revenue ladder.

    Rank projects across impact, speed, confidence, effort and risk. Use it with your leadership team to stop chasing low value ideas and focus on a short list that actually moves NRR and GRR.

    Assess Revenue Impact

  • CSM Workload & Capacity Calculator

    Get real about ratios before your team burns out.

    Map accounts, revenue bands and required activities per segment to see realistic CSM capacity. Use it in headcount requests to show why you need more people or sharper segmentation, not just hero work.

    Calculate CSM Workload
