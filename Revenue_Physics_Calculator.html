How this calculator works
  • Health score → revenue: (Score improvement %) × Base ARR × 0.8
  • Adoption → revenue: (Adoption rate %) × Base ARR × 0.92
  • Onboarding → value: Problem cost × 0.8 × (Efficiency %) × 2.5
